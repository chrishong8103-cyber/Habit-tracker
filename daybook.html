          How to get the file

          1. On your iPhone, open the Health app.
          2. Tap your profile picture (top right), then Export All Health Data → Export. It can take a few minutes.
          3. Choose Save to Files.
          4. Open this page, go to this tab and pick export.zip above.

          Your file is read on your device. Only the daily totals for the days you choose are saved to Daybook, not the whole export. Importing again updates those days and doesn't create duplicates. Sleep you typed in yourself is kept as it is.
